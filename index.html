Voice-based concussion recovery monitoring

The daily vital sign brain recovery never had.

Speakwell turns a 30-second voice sample into an objective recovery curve, from the moment of suspected injury through return-to-play and return-to-work clearance. No hardware beyond a phone. No lab visit. Measured against the person's own baseline, every day, at near-zero marginal cost.

Request a briefing See how it works

Concussion care is broken at both ends of the injury.

Diagnosis is subjective. Clearance is subjective. And the moment that carries the most risk and the most liability, the return-to-play or return-to-work decision, still relies on the same self-reported symptom checklist that failed at the sideline.

At the point of injury

Diagnosis depends on self-report.

Sideline protocols lean on symptom reporting and observer judgment. Athletes underreport to stay in the game. Preseason baseline tests can be sandbagged so a post-injury comparison looks benign.

At the point of clearance

Return-to-play is where second-impact risk lives.

Graduated return-to-play and return-to-work protocols are anchored to the same subjective reporting, at exactly the moment institutional liability concentrates and the cost of getting it wrong is highest.

One measurement engine. Two clinical surfaces.

Speech production is one of the most neurologically demanding motor tasks a person performs. Mild TBI measurably disrupts articulation, timing, coordination, prosody, and fluency — and unlike blood draws, EEG caps, or eye-tracking rigs, voice can be captured anywhere, in under a minute, at near-zero marginal cost.

01

Baseline enrollment

A short standardized voice protocol recorded once at intake or preseason. Measured against the person's own baseline, far harder to sandbag than cognitive tests.

02

Detection screen

A sub-60-second sample compared against baseline at a suspected injury. Decision support, not a replacement for clinical diagnosis.

03 — the flagship surface

Recovery monitoring. Every other tool is episodic; voice is continuous.

Daily 30-second samples from home render an objective recovery trajectory and flag plateaus, regressions, and convergence to baseline — the only modality that can own the recovery arc outright.

Built on a decade of vocal biomarker research.

Machine learning studies of concussion speech have consistently identified discriminative temporal features (duration, rhythm, zero-crossing rate) and spectral features (MFCCs, spectral flatness and bandwidth), with newer work applying deep learning directly to speech spectrograms. Vocal biomarkers already show efficacy in cognitive impairment, Parkinson's, and ALS — adjacent validation for the premise that voice carries clinically meaningful neurological signal.

Status

Speakwell is currently a research and clinical-workflow tool. It records, quantifies, and displays voice metrics over time and does not make diagnostic claims. It is not yet FDA cleared and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Published concussion-specific datasets remain small by ML standards, and real confounds (fatigue, exertion, emotion, ambient noise) are part of why this field is still early — validated enough to build on, not yet mature.
